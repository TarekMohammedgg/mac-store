## Design Context

### Users
Shoppers browsing used Apple devices and accessories (often on phone, Arabic RTL-first) to inspect real photos, condition, and price before visiting or deciding. Primary job: scan inventory quickly and open the right listing with confidence.

### Brand Personality
Calm, precise, trustworthy. The interface should feel like a quiet Apple-adjacent showroom — confident without shouting, sparse without feeling empty.

### Aesthetic Direction
Refined minimal monochrome. Generous whitespace, strong type hierarchy, restrained motion. Light is the default daytime browsing context; dark remains supported. Anti-references: noisy marketplace layouts, purple/cyan AI gradients, heavy multi-shadow cards, pill clusters, and cluttered promo chrome.

### Design Principles
1. **Simplicity first** — enhance hierarchy and presence; never add decorative noise.
2. **Product is the hero** — imagery and price must read immediately; chrome stays quiet.
3. **Bold through contrast, not clutter** — weight, scale, and spacing create emphasis.
4. **One job per surface** — cards exist to compare and click, not to host secondary marketing.
5. **RTL-native** — layouts and motion must feel correct in Arabic first.
